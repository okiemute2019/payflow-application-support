## Evidence quality checklist

- [ ] The scenario is labelled simulated.
- [ ] The screenshot/output is from the actual lab.
- [ ] Secrets and personal data are removed.
- [ ] The evidence supports the written conclusion.
- [ ] The date, environment and relevant reference are recorded.
- [ ] The result can be reproduced.
- [ ] No unverified performance or business-impact claim is included.
